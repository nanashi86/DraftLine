What you get, free

  • Professional formatting

    Tab and Enter move you through every screenplay element. Hollywood pagination with (MORE) and (CONT'D), and a page count that matches the PDF.

  • Story planning

    A Scene Board of index cards in three or four acts, a Story Bible and character bios, beside the script whenever you want them.

  • Polish and prep

    Format Doctor, Dialogue Tuner, snapshots you can compare, and scene breakdowns, a stripboard and reports from the script itself.

  • Yours, everywhere

    No account. Works offline, on a computer or a phone. Final Draft (.fdx) and Fountain in and out, a ready-made PDF, and your scripts stay on your device.
